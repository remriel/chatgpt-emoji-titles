## Short post

I made **Chat Emoji Titles** to make long ChatGPT sidebars easier to scan. Add a topic-relevant emoji to one chat or let it handle visible chats automatically. You can also remove leading emoji without changing the title text. No external AI key or analytics. Source and downloads: https://github.com/remriel/chatgpt-emoji-titles

## Longer community post

If your ChatGPT sidebar has become a wall of similar-looking titles, **Chat Emoji Titles** gives each chat a visual cue. Pick one conversation to add or remove an emoji, or use separate automatic add and remove switches for visible sidebar chats. The add mode uses a small local topic matcher based on the title and selected user messages, with a random fallback. It does not send conversation text to an outside AI service.

The extension is independent of OpenAI. The public source, privacy policy, screenshots, and packaged release are here: https://github.com/remriel/chatgpt-emoji-titles
